#### techfootprint.io
[Matt](https://mattwhite.me) is not one to rest on his laurels. Whilst taking a well-earned breather over the course of this summer, he produced some crucial updates to the LDC Via platform and then he coded an entirely new application, [techfootprint.io](https://techfootprint.io).

As you do.

Over to Matt:

<blockquote cite="https://mattwhite.me/2025/10/07/for-your-consideration/">If you manage more than one AWS account, the cost story gets messy fast. Cost Explorer, CUR, and tags each tell part of the tale, but turning them into a single, explainable narrative takes more time than most weeks allow. I built techfootprint.io to read your AWS data in a safe, read only way and turn it into clear trends, anomaly alerts, and CO2 estimates you can act on in minutes.</blockquote>

You can read more about techfootprint.io, including plans to widen its reach, <a href="https://mattwhite.me/2025/10/07/for-your-consideration/" title="Link to Matt White, “For your consideration”">over at Matt’s site</a>.
