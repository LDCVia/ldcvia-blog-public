Given the <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2025/12/09/us/politics/rubio-state-department-font.html" title="Link to The New York Times, &#8220;At State Dept., a Typeface Falls Victim in the War Against Woke&#8221;">current typography lunacy over there</a>, this post seems rather timely. When [we re-designed the LDC Via site](/2025/11/25/new-website), one of the more subtle changes was our typography. We used to use basic sans-serif fonts, venerable standards like Matt McInerney’s [Raleway](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/raleway), in our documents.

However, some of us (_cough_ Ben _cough_) are typography nerds, and so the re-design also meant a fonts re-think.

We settled on [Inter Tight](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter+Tight), an open-source font designed by Rasmus Andersson. It’s clean and simple. Like us!

If you’re into fonts, there are myriad sites out there, but a few favourites of ours are listed below:

- [The League of Moveable Type](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com)
- [Typography in ten minutes](https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html) which is part of&hellip;
- &hellip; [Butterick’s Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com)
- [How To Spot Arial](https://www.marksimonson.com/notebook/view/how-to-spot-arial/) from Mark Simonson
