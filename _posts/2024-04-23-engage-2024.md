As I type, here in sunny old England (!), the opening general session of [Engage 2024](https://engage.ug) is almost upon us. LDC Via is in attendance – well, three quarters of us! – and we’d be delighted to meet up and chat with any fellow attendees.

This conference is number *fifteen*, and it’s Theo’s last as organiser. We wish him well, and can’t thank him enough for what will no doubt be another wonderful experience: we hope you enjoy your wee gift Theo!

<img src="/assets/img/blog/theo-gift.jpg" height="666px" width="499px" alt="Theo opens his gift" />

For 2024, we’re in the fabulous city of Antwerp, and as always the conference boasts an impressive range of sessions and speakers. Hopefully we will see some of you over the next couple of days!
