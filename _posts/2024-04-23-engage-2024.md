As I type, here in sunny old England (!), the opening general session of [Engage 2024](https://engage.ug) is almost upon us. As always, Engage boasts an impressive range of sessions and speakers. LDC Via is in attendance (well, three quarters of us) and we’d be delighted to meet up and chat with any fellow attendees: hopefully we will see some of you over the next couple of days.

Incidentally, this conference is number *fifteen*, and it’s Theo’s last as organiser. We wish him well, and can’t thank him enough for all that he’s done over the years: we hope you enjoy your wee gift Theo!
