After who knows how long (OK, almost twelve years), we decided to re-do the ole&#8217; website. The more observant out there will have seen that this blog was updated a couple of months back, and we have now followed suit with our main site.

We had some goals for [ldcvia.com](https://ldcvia.com):

- _Strip it back_&#8212;design, but also content
- _Simplify_: less structure, no big builds
- _Speed_: opening the site should be quick
- _Message_: the site needed to reflect what we do in 2025, not what we did in 2014

Now, we&#8217;re no <abbr title="User Interface">UI</abbr> whizzes&#8212;we don&#8217;t know that much about modern <abbr title="Cascading Stylesheets">CSS</abbr>, animations, front-end frameworks and all that&#8212;but then that wasn&#8217;t what we needed. We wanted a clean, simple look to reflect who we are (that said, Ben did get a few CSS animations in there&hellip;).

Job done, hopefully! [Do let us know what you think](mailto:support@ldcvia.com?subject=New%20site%20feedback).
